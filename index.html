Market and nights to come

Chilli Express is a street food stall at a Melbourne night market. Everything comes off the char-grill while you watch: tandoori chicken souvlaki, butter chicken parathas, crisp samosas and a cold mango lassi to put out the fire.

The line is long and it moves fast. There are vegetarian combos on the board too.
